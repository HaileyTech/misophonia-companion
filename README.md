# Misophonia Companion

**A web-based toolkit for sound sensitivity support.**

Misophonia Companion is an Iowa State University College of Liberal Arts and Sciences Honors creative capstone project. It aims to help people better understand and navigate misophonia through accessible educational content, practical tools, personal reflection, and curated support resources.

## Project Goals

- Provide approachable information about misophonia and its potential impact on everyday life.
- Offer practical tools and strategies that visitors can explore at their own pace.
- Provide a private, browser-based journal for personal reflection.
- Connect visitors with relevant research, organizations, and professional resources.

## Features

- **Home:** An overview and starting point for exploring the toolkit.
- **Sound Relief:** Background sound options and information about tools people may find helpful.
- **My Journal:** A space to record personal experiences and reflections.
- **Learn:** Educational content about misophonia, its everyday impact, current research, and coping approaches.
- **Find Support:** A curated collection of questionnaires, support communities, professional directories, and educational resources.
